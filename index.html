Jhouvann Morden is a Filipino computer science student and developer based in Davao City, Philippines, currently enrolled at Mapúa Malayan Colleges of Mindanao (MMCM) . His work spans backend systems and artificial intelligence, with a growing interest in building tools that solve real problems.[citation needed]

While he actively develops software, his strengths and long-term inclination lean toward managerial and coordination roles—handling documentation, structuring workflows, and working with people to ensure systems are not only built, but actually function in real-world contexts.

Morden has held multiple leadership positions within student organizations, including two consecutive terms as Internal Vice President in separate computing societies and a regional auditor role at the provincial level. He is a consistent President's Lister[verified ✓] and recipient of both the CHED and KCPFI scholarships, reflecting sustained academic performance and merit. His technical credentials include the AWS Certified AI Practitioner certification.

He is proficient in Filipino, Cebuano, and English, and documents a typing speed of 90–120 WPM — on a good day.[disputed]

Computer Science student with experience leading technical projects, coordinating cross-functional teams, and applying technology to solve business problems. Skilled in project coordination, stakeholder communication, AI workflow automation, and cloud technologies. Seeking to contribute analytical thinking and organizational skills in technology-driven environments.

Notable projects [edit]

Morden's project work spans conversational AI, economic forecasting, and operational systems, reflecting a tendency to pick whichever problem is interesting and then build something for it anyway.

Most of the projects were developed in collaboration with Marga Pilapil and Mel Macabenta.

AskWhiz — Institutional Knowledge Chatbot

Morden's undergraduate thesis: a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) chatbot that answers institutional questions for MMCM, combining vector search over indexed institutional documents with the Claude API for response generation, evaluated using RAGAS.

FastAPI Amazon OpenSearch Claude API FAISS RAGAS Render

Undergraduate thesis: a RAG chatbot deployed for MMCM, built on FastAPI with OpenSearch/FAISS retrieval and the Claude API.

FastAPI Amazon OpenSearch Claude API FAISS RAGAS Render

EIZOU Creatives — Production Tracker

A full-stack internal tool built for Eizou Films, a content production agency, to manage the entire lifecycle of client deliverables — from planning through production, review, and publishing across social platforms. Replaces what would otherwise be a spreadsheet: tracks monthly deliverable quotas per client, a seven-stage production pipeline, editor workload, deadlines, revision rounds, and multi-platform publish status.

React TypeScript Node.js Express PostgreSQL Prisma

Full-stack production tracker for a content agency — manages client deliverables, editor workload, deadlines, and multi-platform publish status.

React TypeScript Node.js Express PostgreSQL Prisma

Tasa — Coffee-Life Tracker GitHub ↗

A mobile app for logging every cup of coffee — home-brewed or bought at a café — as a lightweight, judgment-free diary, built specifically for the Philippine market with peso-denominated pricing and PH café drinks (Spanish latte, ube latte, sachet 3-in-1) alongside home-brew methods. Originally prototyped as a static HTML mockup before being rebuilt as a real, installable Flutter app, with an Instagram-style feed, a "Rain Check" streak safety net, a shareable Wrapped-style monthly summary, and 46+ badges — fully local, with no backend or accounts.

Flutter Dart Riverpod SQLite

Flutter app for tracking coffee consumption and spend in the Philippines, with streaks, badges, and shareable monthly summaries — fully local, no backend.

Flutter Dart Riverpod SQLite

Margatron — Admissions Buddy Chatbot GitHub ↗

A web-hosted admissions chatbot for MMCM prospective students, featuring natural language processing via the Gemini API and nonsense input filtering for more reliable interaction.

Web-hosted admissions chatbot for MMCM using Gemini API and NLP-based input filtering.

A mobile companion app for real-time kitchen hazard detection targeting elderly users and caregivers. Experience [edit]

Since June 2026, Morden has served as IT/Business Development Associate at Eizou Films in Davao City, a content production startup, splitting his time between technical delivery and client acquisition. On the business development side: generating and qualifying leads through outbound prospecting and market research, managing CRM records and sales pipelines via GoHighLevel, and assisting with marketing strategy, content planning, and client communications.

On the technical side, he built and shipped a WordPress website for a UK-based client, Atlas Waste and Removals, managing domain connection through Squarespace — a project that doubled as a crash course in WordPress itself — and designed and developed the EIZOU Creatives Production Tracker, a full-stack internal tool now used by the agency to run its entire content production pipeline.
